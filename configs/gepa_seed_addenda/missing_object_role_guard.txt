Before adding a large dead-end or missing-object penalty, verify from WINCONDITIONS, RULES, LEGEND aliases/composites, and COLLISIONLAYERS that the object is truly required and no rule or alias can create, transform into, respawn, or reveal it later. If the object can reappear, skip the huge penalty and use finite distance, count progress, or score_normalized only as a low-weight fallback. When using generic keyword role helpers, first check that WINCONDITIONS or RULES actually support that role; otherwise omit those helper terms.
