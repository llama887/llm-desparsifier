Before choosing heuristic terms, construct a compact causal sketch from WINCONDITIONS, RULES, LEGEND aliases, COLLISIONLAYERS, and the initial state: which objects can move, transform, be created, be destroyed, gate access, score, or make progress irreversible. Then decide whether legal path reachability matters because terrain, doors, water, blockers, gravity, beams, swaps, pulling, one-way effects, or other rules change available moves. Use strong counts, Manhattan matching, missing-object checks, and deadlock penalties only when the causal sketch proves they are reliable; otherwise prefer finite low-weight reachable interaction/frontier distances plus score_normalized as a tie-breaker.
