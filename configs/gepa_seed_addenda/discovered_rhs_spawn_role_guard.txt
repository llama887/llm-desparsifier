Before adding any large dead-end or missing-object penalty, first verify from the RULES and LEGEND aliases that the object type never appears on the right-hand side of any rule, meaning it cannot be created later, and that the WINCONDITIONS do not rely on its future creation. If the object can be spawned, skip the penalty and use only distance-based terms or a small score_normalized tie-breaker. Similarly, when using generic keyword-based role helpers, first check that the win-condition text actually references the corresponding role; if the role is not mentioned, omit those helper terms to avoid inflating the heuristic.
